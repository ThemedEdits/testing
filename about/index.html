Who We Are

A Boutique Finance Partner Built Around Senior Expertise

Not an outsourced back office. A dedicated finance function that happens to sit outside your building.

Numeriq Global is an accounting and finance advisory firm built for businesses that have outgrown basic bookkeeping. We combine technical accounting, financial reporting, CFO advisory and financial operations support to help businesses make better decisions.

We work with founders, operators and finance leaders across industries and borders, stepping in as a senior extension of their team rather than a distant vendor sending reports once a month.
