From First Conversation to Ongoing Financial Support

Every engagement starts with understanding your business, not fitting you into a predefined package. From there, we assess what is actually needed, build the right setup, deliver the work, and keep improving it as your business changes.

Understand Assess Build Deliver Improve

The Five Stages of Working Together

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start by understanding your business.

    We take the time to understand your company's structure, current financial processes, challenges, objectives and the level of support you actually need.

    • Understand your business model
    • Review your current accounting setup
    • Identify immediate challenges
    • Understand your reporting and financial goals
  2. 02

    Assess

    We identify what needs attention.

    We review your existing financial environment and determine where improvements, corrections or additional expertise are required.

    • Assess accounting processes
    • Review financial records and reporting
    • Identify gaps and inefficiencies
    • Determine priorities and required support
  3. 03

    Build

    We create the right financial setup.

    We develop the appropriate accounting processes, reporting structure, systems and level of support based on what your business actually requires.

    • Establish or improve accounting workflows
    • Configure reporting structures
    • Set up appropriate systems
    • Define responsibilities and deliverables
  4. 04

    Deliver

    We put the plan into action.

    This is where we deliver the accounting and finance support agreed for your business.

    • Bookkeeping and reconciliations
    • Management reporting
    • Payroll
    • AP & AR
    • Consolidation
    • Technical accounting
    • Forecasting and budgeting
    • Fractional CFO support
  5. 05

    Improve

    We keep your financial function moving forward.

    The relationship does not end once the initial setup is complete. We stay close as your business and its needs continue to change.

    • Ongoing reporting
    • Performance monitoring
    • Financial insights
    • Process improvements
    • Forecasting and planning
    • Adapting support as the business grows
