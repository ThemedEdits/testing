Who Numeriq Is

Your Dedicated Finance Partner, Not Just a Service Provider

Numeriq Global is a boutique financial services firm built for modern businesses that need more from their finance function. We combine technical accounting expertise with practical financial advisory to give founders, CFOs and business leaders numbers they can trust.

From keeping day-to-day books accurate to supporting complex reporting, consolidation and audit readiness, we work as an extension of your team. The goal is simple: make your financial information clearer, more useful and easier to act on.

How We Work

A Clear Financial Process From Day One

  1. 01

    Discovery Call

    We learn about your business, current financial setup, challenges and what you want your finance function to achieve.

  2. 02

    Diagnostic Review

    We review your existing books, systems and reporting to identify gaps, risks and opportunities for improvement.

  3. 03

    Tailored Proposal

    You receive a clear engagement plan covering scope, deliverables, timelines and pricing based on your actual requirements.

  4. 04

    Onboarding & Execution

    We set up the required systems, migrate data where needed and establish clear processes, ownership and reporting rhythms.

  5. 05

    Ongoing Partnership

    Regular reporting, senior access and proactive financial guidance continue as your business grows and your needs change.
